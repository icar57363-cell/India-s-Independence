🇮🇳 FREEDOM FIGHTERS OF INDIA

The brave people who contributed to India's struggle for independence

Mahatma Gandhi

1869–1948

Leader of India's non-violent freedom movement. He led major campaigns including the Non-Cooperation Movement, Civil Disobedience Movement and Quit India Movement.

Subhas Chandra Bose

1897–1945

A powerful nationalist leader who led the Indian National Army and sought to end British rule through armed struggle.

Bhagat Singh

1907–1931

Revolutionary freedom fighter who became a symbol of courage and resistance against British rule.

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

1875–1950

Important Congress leader who participated in the freedom movement and later played a major role in integrating independent India's princely states.

Bal Gangadhar Tilak

1856–1920

Early nationalist leader who promoted self-rule and inspired people through his political writings and movements.

Jawaharlal Nehru

1889–1964

Senior Congress leader who participated in the independence movement and became India's first Prime Minister after 1947.

Lala Lajpat Rai

1865–1928

Nationalist leader known as the Punjab Kesari. He opposed British rule and participated in important nationalist movements.

Chandrashekhar Azad

1906–1931

Revolutionary leader who worked with other revolutionaries against British colonial rule.

Mangal Pandey

1827–1857

Indian soldier whose actions became associated with the beginning of the uprising of 1857.

Bahadur Shah Zafar

1775–1862

The last Mughal emperor. He became a symbolic leader during the uprising of 1857.

Rani Lakshmibai

1828–1858

Queen of Jhansi who became one of the most famous leaders of the uprising of 1857.

Nana Sahib

1824–1859

One of the prominent leaders associated with the uprising of 1857 in the Kanpur region.

Tantia Tope

1814–1859

Skilled military commander who played an important role in the uprising of 1857.

Kunwar Singh

1777–1858

Leader from Bihar who fought against British forces during the uprising of 1857.

Begum Hazrat Mahal

1820–1879

A prominent leader of the 1857 uprising who resisted British rule in Awadh.

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad

1888–1958

Scholar, journalist and Congress leader who strongly supported India's independence movement.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

1891–1956

Major social reformer and jurist who fought against social inequality and later chaired the drafting committee of the Constitution of India.

Dadabhai Naoroji

1825–1917

Early nationalist leader who exposed the economic effects of British colonial rule and became the first Asian British MP.

Gopal Krishna Gokhale

1866–1915

Moderate nationalist leader, social reformer and mentor of Mahatma Gandhi.

Surendranath Banerjee

1848–1925

Early Indian nationalist who helped build political awareness and organized opposition to British policies.

Bipin Chandra Pal

1858–1932

Nationalist leader and powerful speaker who promoted the Swadeshi movement.

Aurobindo Ghosh

1872–1950

Revolutionary nationalist, writer and political thinker who supported India's independence movement.

V. D. Savarkar

1883–1966

Nationalist activist and writer who participated in revolutionary activities against British rule.

Ashfaqulla Khan

1900–1927

Revolutionary associated with the Hindustan Republican Association and the Kakori action.

Ram Prasad Bismil

1897–1927

Revolutionary, poet and founding member of the Hindustan Republican Association.

Rajendra Prasad

1884–1963

Congress leader who participated in the freedom movement and later became the first President of independent India.

Sarojini Naidu

1879–1949

Poet and nationalist leader who participated in the freedom movement and became an important voice for Indian independence.

Annie Besant

1847–1933

Political activist who supported Indian self-government and helped lead the Home Rule movement.

Aruna Asaf Ali

1909–1996

Freedom movement activist remembered especially for her role during the Quit India Movement.

Matangini Hazra

1870–1942

Freedom movement activist who participated in the Quit India Movement and became a symbol of courage.

Usha Mehta

1920–2000

Young freedom activist who helped operate the underground Congress Radio during the Quit India Movement.

Sucheta Kriplani

1908–1974

Freedom fighter and Congress leader who participated in the independence movement.

Alluri Sitarama Raju

1897–1924

Revolutionary leader who led the Rampa Rebellion against British colonial rule.

Birsa Munda

1875–1900

Tribal leader who led a major movement against British colonial rule and exploitation in the Chotanagpur region.

Rani Gaidinliu

1915–1993

Naga freedom fighter who resisted British rule in northeastern India.

Velu Nachiyar

1730–1796

Queen of Sivaganga who fought against the East India Company and is remembered as an early opponent of British expansion.

Kanhu Murmu

19th Century

Along with his brother Sidhu, he led the Santhal rebellion against British rule and exploitation.

Sidhu Murmu

19th Century

Leader of the Santhal rebellion who resisted British authority and exploitative systems.

Kittur Chennamma

1778–1829

Queen of Kittur who led an early armed resistance against the British East India Company.

Begum Rokeya

1880–1932

Social reformer and writer who promoted education and opportunities for women in Indian society.

Udham Singh

1899–1940

Revolutionary who became known for his actions connected to the memory of the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.

Chittaranjan Das

1870–1925

Nationalist leader and lawyer who supported the independence movement and helped establish the Swarajya Party.

Rajguru

1908–1931

Revolutionary who worked alongside Bhagat Singh and Sukhdev in the struggle against British rule.

Sukhdev Thapar

1907–1931

Revolutionary and close associate of Bhagat Singh who worked with the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association.

Madam Bhikaji Cama

1861–1936

Nationalist who worked internationally for India's independence and promoted the Indian cause abroad.

Lakshmi Sahgal

1914–2012

Member of the Indian National Army who commanded the Rani of Jhansi Regiment under Subhas Chandra Bose.

Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan

1890–1988

Leader of the Khudai Khidmatgar movement who supported non-violent resistance against British rule.

Maulana Hasrat Mohani

1875–1951

Poet and nationalist who advocated complete independence for India.

Jatindra Nath Das

1904–1929

Revolutionary who participated in the freedom movement and became known for his hunger strike in prison.

Batukeshwar Dutt

1910–1965

Revolutionary who worked with Bhagat Singh and participated in the Central Legislative Assembly protest.

Surya Sen

1894–1934

Revolutionary leader known for organizing the Chittagong Armoury Raid in 1930.

Vallabhbhai Patel

1875–1950

A major Congress leader who organized mass movements and played an important role in the independence struggle.

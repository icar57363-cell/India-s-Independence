🇮🇳 FREEDOM FIGHTERS OF INDIA

The brave people who contributed to India's freedom struggle

Mahatma Gandhi

1869–1948

Leader of India's non-violent freedom movement. He led major campaigns including the Non-Cooperation Movement, Civil Disobedience Movement and Quit India Movement.

Subhas Chandra Bose

1897–1945

A major nationalist leader who led the Indian National Army and fought for India's independence from British rule.

Bhagat Singh

1907–1931

Revolutionary freedom fighter who became a symbol of courage and resistance against British rule.

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

1875–1950

Important Congress leader who participated in the freedom movement and later helped integrate India's princely states.

Jawaharlal Nehru

1889–1964

Senior Congress leader who participated in the independence movement and became India's first Prime Minister.

Bal Gangadhar Tilak

1856–1920

Early nationalist leader who strongly promoted self-rule and inspired people through his political work.

Lala Lajpat Rai

1865–1928

Nationalist leader known as the Punjab Kesari who opposed British rule and supported India's freedom movement.

Chandrashekhar Azad

1906–1931

Revolutionary leader who worked with other revolutionaries against British colonial rule.

Rani Lakshmibai

1828–1858

Queen of Jhansi who became one of the most famous leaders of the uprising of 1857.

Mangal Pandey

1827–1857

Indian soldier whose actions became associated with the uprising of 1857 against British rule.

Begum Hazrat Mahal

1820–1879

Important leader of the 1857 uprising who resisted British rule in Awadh.

Tantia Tope

1814–1859

Military commander who played an important role in the uprising of 1857.

Kunwar Singh

1777–1858

Leader from Bihar who fought against British forces during the uprising of 1857.

Maulana Abul Kalam Azad

1888–1958

Scholar, journalist and Congress leader who strongly supported India's independence movement.

Rajendra Prasad

1884–1963

Congress leader who participated in the freedom movement and later became India's first President.

Sarojini Naidu

1879–1949

Poet and nationalist leader who participated actively in India's freedom movement.

Aruna Asaf Ali

1909–1996

Freedom movement activist remembered especially for her role during the Quit India Movement.

Usha Mehta

1920–2000

Young freedom activist who helped operate the underground Congress Radio during the Quit India Movement.

Ram Prasad Bismil

1897–1927

Revolutionary, poet and member of the Hindustan Republican Association.

Ashfaqulla Khan

1900–1927

Revolutionary associated with the Hindustan Republican Association and the Kakori action.

Rajguru

1908–1931

Revolutionary who worked alongside Bhagat Singh and Sukhdev in the struggle against British rule.

Sukhdev Thapar

1907–1931

Revolutionary and associate of Bhagat Singh who worked against British colonial rule.

Udham Singh

1899–1940

Revolutionary remembered for his actions connected to the memory of the Jallianwala Bagh massacre.

Surya Sen

1894–1934

Revolutionary leader known for organizing the Chittagong Armoury Raid in 1930.

Alluri Sitarama Raju

1897–1924

Revolutionary leader who led the Rampa Rebellion against British colonial rule.

Birsa Munda

1875–1900

Tribal leader who led a major movement against British colonial rule and exploitation.

Rani Gaidinliu

1915–1993

Naga freedom fighter who resisted British rule in northeastern India.

Kittur Chennamma

1778–1829

Queen of Kittur who led an early armed resistance against the British East India Company.

Velu Nachiyar

1730–1796

Queen of Sivaganga who fought against the East India Company and resisted British expansion.

Madam Bhikaji Cama

1861–1936

Nationalist who worked internationally for India's independence and promoted the Indian cause abroad.

Lakshmi Sahgal

1914–2012

Member of the Indian National Army who commanded the Rani of Jhansi Regiment under Subhas Chandra Bose.

Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan

1890–1988

Leader of the Khudai Khidmatgar movement who supported non-violent resistance against British rule.

Gopal Krishna Gokhale

1866–1915

Moderate nationalist leader, social reformer and an important influence on Mahatma Gandhi.

Dadabhai Naoroji

1825–1917

Early nationalist leader who highlighted the economic effects of British colonial rule.

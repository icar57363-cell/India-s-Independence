INDIA'S JOURNEY

The Road to Independence 🇮🇳

1857

The First Major Uprising

The uprising of 1857 became an important moment in India's resistance to British rule. It brought together people from different regions and became an important part of the story of India's freedom.

1885

A New National Movement

The Indian National Congress was founded in 1885. It became an important platform for political discussion and later played a major role in the independence movement.

1905

The Swadeshi Movement

The Swadeshi movement encouraged people to support Indian-made goods and promote self-reliance. It became an important expression of national consciousness.

1919

A Turning Point

The events of 1919 deeply affected India's freedom movement. They increased public opposition to colonial rule and strengthened the demand for greater freedom.

1920

Non-Cooperation Movement

A nationwide movement encouraged Indians to peacefully withdraw cooperation from British institutions. It brought many ordinary people into the national movement.

1930

The Salt March

The Salt March became a powerful symbol of peaceful resistance. The movement challenged British policies and attracted worldwide attention.

1942

Quit India Movement

The Quit India Movement called for an end to British rule. It became one of the major mass movements of India's struggle for independence.

1947

The Final Chapter

After decades of struggle, India reached a historic turning point. The country was preparing to become an independent nation.

15 AUGUST 1947 🇮🇳

INDIA BECAME INDEPENDENT
